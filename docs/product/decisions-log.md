# Decisions log

Every resolved product and engineering question, with the answer, who decided it, when, and where it is implemented. Open and deferred items are in [backlog.md](backlog.md).

"Founder" means a founder decision. "Engineering" means a default the team applied and shipped without a founder decision; it stands until a founder decision or a decision record changes it. Versions name the release that first shipped the behavior.
