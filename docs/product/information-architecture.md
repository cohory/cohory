# Information architecture

How Cohory is organized for members, creators, and admins: screens, URLs, navigation rules, and where each action lives.

This document covers the web application in the MVP and notes where later phases change it. Visual details (color, type, spacing) live in [../brand/design-tokens.md](../brand/design-tokens.md). Permission rules behind each role live in [../architecture/authorization.md](../architecture/authorization.md). Phases refer to [../foundation/roadmap.md](../foundation/roadmap.md).

## 1. Structure at a glance

The product has two levels. The platform level belongs to a person (their account and the communities they belong to). The community level belongs to one community (a tenant) and looks the same in every community.

```
PLATFORM
├── My Communities            list of communities I own or belong to
├── Create Community          short wizard
└── Account                   profile, login, notifications, memberships and billing
    
COMMUNITY  (/c/{slug})
├── Community                 member tab 1
│   ├── Feed
│   └── Post details
├── Classroom                 member tab 2
│   ├── Courses
│   ├── Course detail
│   └── Lesson
├── Calendar                  member tab 3
│   ├── Month / List
│   └── Event details
├── Members                   member tab 4
│   ├── Directory
│   ├── Profile
│   └── Leaderboard
├── About                     non-member tab 1 (people who are not members; members open it only by URL)
└── Manage                    owner and admins only (moderators see Inbox and People only)
    ├── Overview
    ├── Inbox
    ├── People
    ├── Payments
    ├── Appearance
    ├── Settings
    │   └── Webhooks          owner and admins (since 0.6.0)
    └── Migration             owner; admins see Bring your existing subscribers only
```

**Confirmed:** members see exactly four tabs: Community, Classroom, Calendar, Members. Since Step 17c (founder decision H4, 2026-10-20) people who are not members of a public community see About first, then the same four tabs; a private community shows them only About, with no tabs (see [ADR 0026](../decisions/0026-community-page-for-non-members.md)). Creators manage the community in a separate Manage area with Overview, Inbox, People, Payments, Appearance, Settings, and Migration.

**Confirmed** (founder decision, 2026-10-19): Migration is the sixth Manage section (the last of seven since Inbox was added in the review of 2026-10-21), at `/c/{slug}/manage/migration`, and members keep exactly four tabs. Up to 0.8.0 it was a card inside Settings; the old URLs `/c/{slug}/manage/settings/migration` and `/c/{slug}/manage/settings/migration/subscribers` now redirect permanently to the new ones. For the owner the section shows, in this order since r39: Import from another platform, Import members from a spreadsheet, Import history (once an import has run), Bring your existing subscribers, and under a "Cohory archives" heading Export archive and Import archive. Export archive: start, progress, download, history with what each export holds and how long it is kept (30 days), and Delete. Import archive: upload, preview, confirm, progress, result. Running jobs show the step with a count, how long the job has run, and that the page can be closed. Choose other files discards a preview and its stored file. Import history lists each finished or failed import with source, date, counts, rows skipped, emails sent, and Download report (CSV); an import whose emails wait shows "Emails not sent yet" and **Send the emails**. Before confirming an import the owner answers **Email these people?** (Email everyone now, I will send emails later, Do not email), with "Note from you (optional)", **Preview email**, and **Send me a test** ([0043](../decisions/0043-import-invitations-and-emails.md)); People shows **Resend email** to the owner for an imported person who has not got in yet. Import from another platform (since 0.4.0, P2b: pick Skool or Circle, upload several export files, see what each file was detected as, a preview with counts, warnings, rows and columns left out, and a link to the source's limits page, then confirm, progress, result; since r22 the preview also shows roles, category mapping, a sample post, people numbers that match the result, the date order used, a choice of member for author names that more than one member has, "Use {platform} instead" when the files belong to the other platform (Import is off while most files are not recognized), and "Download rows to fix (CSV)", and the result lists the rows the files left out), Import members from a spreadsheet (CSV upload, row preview with an "Only rows to fix" switch and "Download rows to fix (CSV)", invite or add, result; people with an open invitation from an earlier upload show as Invited and are skipped unless the owner turns on "Send again", the owner's own row shows as "This is you.", and rows whose status is not active are left out), and a Bring your existing subscribers card that opens `/c/{slug}/manage/migration/subscribers` (since 0.4.0, P2a). That page links existing subscriptions on the Community Edition only and, on every edition, holds Paying members to move: a paste form, "Add them to the list" after an import, the list with each person's state and free until date, and "Email everyone not moved yet" ([0042](../decisions/0042-moving-paying-members.md)). Admins, who may link subscriptions but not export or import (export, import, and the members CSV stay owner only, founder decision 2026-10-19), see the section with only the Bring your existing subscribers card and a line saying the rest is for the owner. Moderators do not see the section.

**Recommendation:** a signed-in person with exactly one community lands directly in that community instead of My Communities. A person with none lands on My Communities with an empty state that offers "Create a community" and explains that invite links open communities directly.

As built ([0033](../decisions/0033-platform-config-and-neutral-home.md), [0047](../decisions/0047-cloud-marketing-in-the-app.md)): `/` redirects a signed in person with exactly one community to `/c/{slug}`. `/?home=1` always shows My communities, and the Home item in the avatar menu links there. When the operator sets `HOME_COMMUNITY`, signed out visitors go to that community's About page instead. Otherwise signed out `/` depends on the edition. On the Community Edition it is a neutral page: "Welcome to {instance name}", one line ("Sign in to see your communities, or create your own."), and one button, Create your community, which opens `/sign-up?next=/communities/new`; Sign in stays in the platform bar only, and the page names no fee and carries no sales copy. The platform bar and page titles use the instance name and logo (`INSTANCE_NAME`, `INSTANCE_LOGO_URL`), which default to Cohory and its icon.

### 4.1 Custom domains

**Recommendation (Cloud and self-hosted):** a custom domain maps to one community root. `learn.example.com/` serves `/c/{slug}`, `learn.example.com/classroom/{courseSlug}/{lessonSlug}` serves `/c/{slug}/classroom/{courseSlug}/{lessonSlug}`, and so on. The `/c/{slug}` form keeps working and redirects to the custom domain once the domain is verified. Account pages and checkout stay reachable on the custom domain so members are not bounced to another host. Post-MVP (founder decision, 2026-10-25); not built yet.

**Recommendation (self-hosted):** an instance can set a default community, so a single-community install serves that community at `/` without a custom domain step. As built, `HOME_COMMUNITY` sends signed out visitors who open `/` to that community's About page.

**Open question:** how sessions work across the platform domain and many custom domains (separate cookie per domain with a sign-in handoff, or a single sign-on redirect). This belongs in [../architecture/authorization.md](../architecture/authorization.md).

## 6. The five UX rules

| Rule | Statement | Rationale | How we check it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maximum four main navigation tabs for members | Members of several communities learn the layout once. Fewer tabs mean fewer decisions and a bottom bar that fits a small phone. | Design review rejects any fifth tab. |
| 2 | One primary action per page, no competing buttons | A screen with one obvious next step is faster to use and easier to learn. Secondary actions stay visible but quieter (ghost or text buttons, menus). | Each screen in section 7 has one filled accent button at most. |
| 3 | Create content where it lives | Creators already think "I want to add a lesson to this course". Sending them to an admin panel breaks that thought and duplicates screens. | No "create post", "create lesson", or "create event" forms in Manage. |
| 4 | No unnecessary settings, use sensible defaults and progressive disclosure | Every setting is a decision a creator must understand. Good defaults let a community launch with zero configuration. | A new community works with only a name and slug. Each new setting needs a written reason in its PR. |
| 5 | Mobile-first simplicity, bottom navigation for the four tabs | Many members read and post from phones. Designing for the small screen first keeps the hierarchy honest. | Every MVP screen is designed and tested at 375 px width first. |

**Confirmed:** these five rules come from the founder notes, along with these principles: a member can create a post in one or two interactions, a member can resume a course from the community homepage, mobile uses the same core information hierarchy.

## 8. Progressive disclosure

Hidden by default, shown when needed:

- **Composer:** collapsed to a single "Write something" field. Expands to title, body, category, image button, and Post. Since 0.6.0, a calendar button opens a "Publish on" date and time field in the member's own time zone (the zone name is shown); the main button then schedules the post instead of posting it. Formatting tools appear only on text selection or via a single "Aa" toggle. On phones the action bar sits above the bottom tabs. Typed text is saved on this device and offered back as a draft (see [user-journeys](user-journeys.md) journey 5).
- **Post menu:** edit, delete, pin, move category, report, and "Email to all members" live in an overflow menu. Only actions the role allows are shown.
- **Lesson sidebar:** open on desktop, collapsed on mobile and in focus mode.
- **Course editing:** members never see edit controls. Admins see a single "Edit" toggle on Course detail that reveals drag handles, add buttons, and draft badges.
- **Event options:** the New event form shows title, date, time, duration, and location. Recurrence, description, and cover image are behind "More options".
- **Settings:** as built since 0.11.0, each Settings group is its own view behind the Settings navigation (section 3.2). Danger zone is last and owner only.
- **Archived communities:** an archived community is read only. Every tab shows a banner that says so. Members can read posts, comments, courses, lessons, and events, but no one can post, comment, like, RSVP, record lesson progress, or edit courses and events. Managers keep Settings (including restore, transfer, and delete) and the members CSV export, but People and Appearance changes and new invite links are refused until the owner restores the community. Joining is blocked. Services enforce this with a `community_archived` error (HTTP 409). New paid memberships are closed too (since Step 18): the membership page shows "This community is archived, so new memberships are closed." instead of a Join button, while paying members can still open Manage billing.
- **Payments:** pricing fields appear only after Stripe is connected. Annual price appears behind "Add an annual price".
- **Gamification:** level thresholds are fixed in MVP and not shown in settings. Level names are editable inside Settings.
- **Technical details:** **Recommendation**, not built as a disclosure. Webhook status, the Stripe account, and API information should stay off the main surface of Payments. As built, the connection status card shows the account and its state in plain words.

**Recommendation:** never use a setting to avoid a product decision. If a default works for most communities, ship the default and no toggle.

| Event | Calendar | "New event" on Calendar | Admins and owner |
| Membership pricing | Manage, Payments | Pricing card | Owner (admins: Open question) |
| Appearance | Manage, Appearance | Section form | Owner and admins |

**Recommendation:** moderators cannot create courses or events in MVP. **Open question:** whether creators want a separate "teacher" role for Classroom only. Not in MVP.

## 10. Notifications placement

- **Recommendation:** a bell icon in the top bar, scoped to the current community, with a small badge for unread count. A "All communities" switch inside the panel shows notifications across communities. My Communities cards show an unread dot.
- **Recommendation:** MVP in-app notification types: reply to my post, reply to my comment, mention, likes on my content (grouped, "Ana and 4 others liked your post"), new event in a community I belong to, event starting soon (RSVP'd only), join request approved, new join request (admins), payment failed (the member and the owner).
- **Recommendation:** MVP email notifications: mentions and replies (batched, at most one email per 15 minutes per person), event reminder one hour before start (RSVP'd only), join request approved, receipts and payment problems, and creator announcements ("Email to all members" on a post, at most once per 24 hours per community).
- **Recommendation:** preferences live in Account, Notifications, per community, with three levels per channel: All, Important only (mentions, replies, payments, events I RSVP'd to), None. Transactional emails (receipts, security) cannot be turned off.
- Since 0.3.0: preferences are per type for every community at once, not per community. Each email links to Account, Notifications and to a confirm page at `/unsubscribe` that turns that type off without signing in.
- Since 0.11.0 ([0034](../decisions/0034-notification-types-and-weekly-digest.md)): the owner and admins get "{name} and N others joined the community"; authors get likes grouped per post or comment; the owner, admins, and moderators get new reports grouped per hour; the owner gets one notice with the price for a new paying member ("Sam joined and pays $49.00/month", without a separate join notice), cancellations, and the first failed payment of a member. These open Manage, People or its Reports tab. Members get an email for each of Stripe's first three attempts when a renewal fails. The bell shows the first line of the comment or post under mentions, replies, and comments (left out for a member without access to the community's content), and "Show older notifications" at the end of the list.
- Since 0.11.0: right away emails wait 3 minutes and merge what is pending per post, so a mention and a reply on one post arrive as one email titled by the post ("2 updates on {post title}"), and join requests arriving together become one email per community ("3 people asked to join"). Each email names the action in one sentence, quotes the comment, the join answer, or the decision note, and has a button for its type (Open the post, Open the comment, Review the request, Open {community}, Review the approval, Read the review, Open your agents). The owner gets join requests right away; admins and moderators get them in the daily summary unless they choose otherwise. Quotes are left out when the post or comment was removed or the reader lost access by send time, and a join request email goes only to someone who may still approve it. A post without a title gets the subject "New activity on a post ({last 4 characters of its id})" so every email about it threads together. Unsubscribing from daily summaries also stops join request emails for someone who helps run a community and never chose a setting for them; the unsubscribe page says "If you help run a community, join request emails stop too." This replaces the 15 minute batch recommended above.
- Since 0.11.0: a weekly digest email on Monday at the member's digest hour, one email for all their communities, with its own switch in Account, Notifications under the daily summary. A community the member can no longer read is left out of it.
- Post-MVP: web push, mobile push (Long-term with native apps).

## 11. Search placement

- As built: a search box in the platform bar (since Step 17c; it was in the community header before), or on phones an icon that opens a search dialog with the same field, opens `/c/{slug}/search` with three result groups: Posts (title, body, comments), Members (name, username, bio), Classroom (lesson title and body). Results respect access: deleted posts are left out, members need the member list permission, and only published lessons in published courses appear to people who may open lessons. Each word matches as a prefix, and excerpts highlight the matches.
- **Recommendation:** MVP uses PostgreSQL full-text search. No cross-community search for members.
- Members directory has its own inline search because finding a person is the primary job of that screen.
- Post-MVP: event search, filters by date and author.

## 13. Roles visible in the UI

| Role | Who | What changes in the UI |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | One person per community (the creator) | Full Manage access including Payments, transfer ownership, delete. "Owner" badge on profile. |
| Admin | Trusted helpers chosen by the owner | Manage access except pricing, the payment connection, refunds, ownership transfer, and deletion. Can grant free access and link existing subscriptions in Payments. Creates courses, events, categories, pins posts. "Admin" badge. |
| Moderator | Community helpers | Edit, delete, or move any post or comment, approve join requests, remove members (not ban admins). Manage shows Inbox and People only. "Mod" badge. |
| Member | Anyone with an active membership | Four tabs, post, comment, like, RSVP, learn. No badge. |
| Guest | Signed out or signed in without membership | The join hero on every page. About only (private community), or About, the read only feed and Calendar, and join prompts in Classroom and Members (public community, since Step 17c). Primary action is Join. |

Roles are per community. A person can be owner of one community and member of another. **Recommendation:** pending applicants are guests with a "Request pending" banner on the About page. Banned people see the About page with no Join button. Agents appear as their own identities with an "Agent" badge and scoped permissions, never as a role that grants unrestricted financial or admin access. Since 0.6.0: posts and comments written by an agent show the author (the agent's owner) followed by an "Agent" badge whose tooltip and screen reader text name the agent; the audit log shows the agent name, the badge, and "for" the owner. See [../architecture/authorization.md](../architecture/authorization.md).

## 15. Decisions and open questions

Confirmed:
- Four member tabs: Community, Classroom, Calendar, Members. Bottom navigation on mobile. Non-members of a public community see About first, then the same four (founder decision H4, 2026-10-20).
- Manage sections: Overview, Inbox, People, Payments, Appearance, Settings, Migration (Inbox added after the review of 2026-10-21). Creators only; moderators see Inbox and People.
- Content is created where it lives. Plain-language creator settings. The five UX rules.
- Creator customization is limited to accent color, logo, cover image, 3 curated fonts, card style, and appearance mode. Layout stays the same.

Recommendation:
- URL scheme under `/c/{slug}`, with custom domains mapping the community root (custom domains are Post-MVP and not built yet).
- Feed is the community home at `/c/{slug}` and carries the "Continue learning" card.
- Tabs are never hidden, even when empty.
- Admins cannot change pricing, the payment connection, or refunds, and cannot delete the community (as built: `payment.edit_plan`, `payment.connect`, and `payment.refund` are owner only in `@cohory/authz`). Moderators see Inbox and People only.
- Notification bell scoped to the current community. Right away emails merge what arrives within 3 minutes per post (section 10).
- PostgreSQL full-text search, community-scoped.

Assumption:
- Most members use one to three communities, so the Your communities list in the avatar menu is enough (no sidebar of community icons).
- Video is hosted elsewhere (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia) for MVP.

Open question:
- Session handling across custom domains.
- Whether creators may hide Calendar.
- Whether a Classroom-only teacher role is needed.
