# Slop patterns

Scan for these and rewrite. Preserve meaning and tone.

## Content

- **Superficial -ing phrases.** "highlighting...", "ensuring...", "showcasing...", "fostering...". Delete, or state the fact.
- **Vague attributions.** "Experts believe", "industry reports suggest". Name the source or delete.
- **Generic conclusions.** "The future looks bright." State the plan or the fact.

## Vocabulary

- **AI words.** additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, robust, seamless, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant. Use plain words.
- **Fancy "is".** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Say "is" or "has".
- **Plain word over fancy.** utilize and leverage become use, facilitate becomes help, numerous becomes many, in the event that becomes if.
- **Abstract metaphor nouns.** substrate, wedge, vector (meaning way), locus, nexus, primitive as a noun, harness as a metaphor, surface as in "API surface", bedrock, scaffolding as a metaphor, modality, paradigm, north star, flywheel, endgame. Pick the concrete word.

## Structure

- **"Not just X, but Y."** State the point directly.
- **Forced groups of three.** Use the natural count.
- **Synonym cycling.** Pick one name and repeat it.
- **False ranges.** "from X to Y" where X and Y are not on a scale. List the items.

## Punctuation and formatting

- **Dashes.** No em dashes, en dashes, or spaced hyphens as punctuation. Use a period, a comma, or parentheses.
- **Colons as connectors.** Fine before a list or example. Not as a mid-sentence hinge.
- **Bold overuse.** Do not bold every term.
- **Inline-header lists.** "**Performance:** Performance improved..." restates itself. Convert to prose. A bold lead-in that ends in a period and introduces new detail is fine.
- **Title Case Headings.** Use sentence case.
- **Emojis.** Remove.
- **Curly quotes.** Use straight quotes.

## Communication tells

- **Chatbot phrases.** "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Certainly!", "Great question!", "You're absolutely right!" Remove.
- **Filler.** "Due to the fact that" becomes "because". Delete "it is important to note that".
- **Hedging.** "could potentially possibly" becomes "may".

## Plain speech

- **Say what it does, not how it feels.** "Types that follow your schema" names a feeling. "A column rename fails the build" names a mechanism. If the sentence could appear unchanged in another project's docs, cut it.
- **Split dense sentences.** If the reader backtracks to parse it, break it in two.
- **Active voice.** "Queries are validated" becomes "zod validates the query input".
- **Cut adverbs.** "Significantly improves" becomes the measured delta.
- **Mannered prose.** Aphorisms, personified code ("the plan holds it"), figurative verbs. Say it literally.
- **Over-compression.** Dropped articles, arrows, and symbol-speak. "Parser rejects bad date → exit 2" becomes "The parser rejects a bad date and exits with code 2."
